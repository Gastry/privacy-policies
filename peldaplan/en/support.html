What to include in a support request

Include the PeldaPlan version, device model and Android version, steps to reproduce the problem and units used. If needed, attach a screenshot or sample project without sensitive data. There is no automatic remote access to your projects.

Keeping your projects safe

In Projects, open Backups to save a JSON backup on your device or to Google Drive if it is available in Android’s file picker. For optional automatic sync, open Drive sync and activate PRO. Restoring creates new projects. Previous versions lets you recover up to ten saved states. Keep an external backup before uninstalling or changing devices.

Understanding the calculations

The guide explains riser height, tread depth, total going, walking lines, winders, stringers and templates. The app calculates geometry; it does not check structure or regulatory compliance. If a measurement differs from another tool, include your inputs and the top-floor or landing convention in your enquiry. Before using a template, check its print scale and actual dimensions.

Languages and platforms

The Android app is available in Spanish, English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Polish, Turkish, Indonesian, Japanese and Korean. You can change the language in Information and help. No iOS version is announced.

Drive sync

Google Drive sync requires PRO and the user’s explicit activation. PRO is a one-time Google Play purchase with no automatic renewal; the price and terms appear before confirmation. Availability depends on the store and this installation. You can turn off sync while keeping local data and Drive copies. Recovery is not guaranteed if an upload has not completed. Keep external backups too. Google handles storage and purchases under its terms and privacy policy.

Choose what happens to future deletions. Changing this setting does not affect previous deletions or exported JSON backups.

Android decides when background syncing runs. Without a connection, free space in Drive or permission to access it, changes stay pending. Check the last sync before uninstalling or changing phones, and keep a local backup.
