Privacy policy

1. Publisher and contact

The publisher and support contact is Juan Mauricio Piano Monzó, publishing as Gastry. You can write to thegastry@gmail.com with the subject “PeldaPlan privacy”.

2. Data processed on your device

The app uses the dimensions and names you enter, your projects, up to ten previous versions per project and your chosen unit of measurement. These are stored locally in Android’s private app storage. Temporary reports and backups are prepared there too. Avoid adding unnecessary personal information to names or files.

Privacy

PeldaPlan has no advertising, analytics or servers of its own. It uses the Internet for optional Google Drive sync and Google Play purchase verification. The selected account and sync status are stored on the device; Google handles authorization and purchases. The publisher receives neither projects nor credentials. PeldaPlan does not add its own encryption.

4. Files you choose to export or import

When exporting PDF, SVG, CSV or JSON, you choose the destination using Android’s file picker. If you choose a cloud provider such as Google Drive, or later share a file, that provider may process the data under its own terms. This does not enable PeldaPlan’s own synchronisation. Import reads the file you select and validates it before adding local copies of the projects.

5. Retention, security and deletion

Projects remain on your device until you delete them, clear the list, clear app data or uninstall the app. Deleting a project also removes its local versions. Preferences and private temporary files are removed when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Exported backups stay where you saved them; you must delete those separately. This version disables Android-managed automatic backup and device transfer. It relies on private storage isolation and device security, adds no database encryption and cannot guarantee recovery without an external backup.

6. Voluntary support requests

If you send an email, the publisher receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to send, to reply and handle your request. Support uses Gmail and is also subject to that service’s terms. Information is kept as needed to answer your enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Do not send passwords, keys or files containing unnecessary personal data.

7. This website and external services

Documentation is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open the website, GitHub may process your IP address and technical request data under its privacy policy. This site adds no analytics, advertising, forms, remote fonts or non-essential cookies of its own. Links to GitHub, Google Play, email or authorities open services with their own policies; they are not tracking services built into PeldaPlan.

8. Your rights and control over your data

Project data exists only on your device or in backups you have made, so the publisher cannot view or delete it remotely. Manage it from Projects and Android settings. For data you have sent to support, you may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability where applicable by writing to thegastry@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or the competent supervisory authority. The publisher does not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

Drive sync

Google Drive sync requires PRO and the user’s explicit activation. PRO is a one-time Google Play purchase with no automatic renewal; the price and terms appear before confirmation. Availability depends on the store and this installation. You can turn off sync while keeping local data and Drive copies. Recovery is not guaranteed if an upload has not completed. Keep external backups too. Google handles storage and purchases under its terms and privacy policy.

Choose what happens to future deletions. Changing this setting does not affect previous deletions or exported JSON backups.

Android decides when background syncing runs. Without a connection, free space in Drive or permission to access it, changes stay pending. Check the last sync before uninstalling or changing phones, and keep a local backup.

How PeldaPlan uses Google Drive and Google Play

When you turn on sync, PeldaPlan requests only the drive.appdata permission. Saved projects and up to ten earlier versions are sent over HTTPS to PeldaPlan's private, hidden folder in your Drive; this permission does not allow the app to browse your other files. The app stores the selected account's email address and a derived account identifier on your device to keep accounts separate. Projects are not sent to the publisher's servers.

If you choose to sync deletions, the affected versions are removed from Drive once sync completes. Technical deletion markers without project contents may be retained to prevent another device from bringing a deleted project back. If you choose to keep projects in Drive, deleting them locally does not remove them from Drive. Turning off sync or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete Drive data. You can revoke access through your Google Account.

Google Play handles payments. PeldaPlan stores the purchase receipt and licence status to verify and restore PRO; it does not receive card numbers. The publisher does not sell this data or use it for advertising. Manually exported JSON backups are separate from sync.
