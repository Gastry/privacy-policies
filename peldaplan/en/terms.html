Using the tool

StairFolio lets you explore stair geometry and keep projects on your device. Its current functions are those described in this version’s listing. Pending functions are not supplied as available capabilities. The EULA sets out the permissions for using the software.

Measurements and construction decisions

Check your inputs, units and calculation conventions. Reports describe geometry and do not replace checks of structure, regulations, supports, connections or fabrication. Have a competent professional confirm the design before construction work or making parts. Exported files identify the geometric scope of the results.

Projects and files

You can save, edit, export and import your projects. Import creates copies with new identifiers; compatibility with other apps’ formats is not promised. The publisher cannot access local projects or recover them remotely. Clearing data or uninstalling removes the app’s private information, although externally exported files may remain.
