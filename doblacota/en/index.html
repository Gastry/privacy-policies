Privacy policy

Version 1.0.0 · Updated: 7 November 2026
Android · com.aplicaciones.gastry.doblacota

Publisher: Juan Mauricio Piano Monzó
Contact: thegastry@gmail.com

Data kept on your device

Calculations are saved on your device first. Drive merges changes when you are online; Android may delay background syncing.

Only calculation records and deletion markers are transferred to your selected Google account. Your Pro receipt, authorization data and preferences stay on the device. Files in the hidden folder cannot be shared.

Connections and third parties

Pro uses Google Play Billing for the one-time purchase and Play Feature Delivery to download the additional module. Purchasing, restoring purchases and downloading require an internet connection and the device’s Google Play account. Google handles payment; the app does not receive your card details. The publisher does not run a licence server or receive your dimensions or calculation sheets. The app contains no advertising, publisher-operated analytics or custom diagnostic SDK.

According to Google’s documentation, module delivery processes device and app-version metadata to deliver the correct module and retain it during updates. Google Play processes store and purchase data under its own terms and privacy policy. Links to Google’s privacy policy and Play Feature Delivery’s data-safety information appear below.

Google Drive · Pro. Backup is optional. Connecting merges this device’s saved calculations with those in the selected account.

You can revoke access in your Google account and delete Doblacota data in Drive’s app settings. Other connected devices may upload changes again.

Jobs contain the title you enter, input measurements, calculation method, units, bender or radius, and version and date metadata. Titles may contain private information; avoid unnecessary personal details. The app stores the selected account email and a technical account reference locally to keep synchronization queues separate. It does not send this data to a publisher-operated server.

Google processes authorization and storage requests under its own terms and privacy policy. Access is limited to Doblacota files in appDataFolder and cannot read the rest of your Drive. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Copying, exporting and contacting support

Copying a sheet writes to the Android clipboard. Exporting uses the system file picker and saves text to the destination you choose. If you select an installed cloud provider, its terms and privacy policy apply to the file you save there. The app does not gain general access to your files.

If you choose to email support, the publisher receives your message and attachments to answer your enquiry. Do not attach information you do not want to share. The app does not send messages or diagnostics automatically. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry. You may request their deletion at the same email address, unless retention is legally required.

Retention and deletion

Also delete from Drive: Deletion will sync to Drive and other connected devices when online. A different version created offline will be kept to prevent data loss.

Keep in Drive: The calculation will be deleted only from this device. Its Drive copy and copies on other devices will be kept. Syncing will not make it reappear here; you can recover it.

Recovery creates a new calculation and keeps your current ones. These versions are stored on this device, including those received from Drive.

There are no ads or analytics. Google Play manages Pro. You can delete calculations and separately erase recovery history. Uninstalling removes local data but not Drive copies.
