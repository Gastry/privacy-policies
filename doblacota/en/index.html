Privacy policy

Version 1.0.0 · Updated: 6 November 2026
Android · com.aplicaciones.gastry.doblacota

Publisher: Juan Mauricio Piano Monzó
Contact: thegastry@gmail.com

Data kept on your device

Doblacota stores preferences, your last valid dimensions, calculation sheets and, if you activate Pro, a signed purchase receipt in this installation’s private storage. It does not ask for an app account, name, phone number, location, contacts or advertising identifiers. The publisher does not receive this data.

Connections and third parties

Pro uses Google Play Billing for the one-time purchase and Play Feature Delivery to download the additional module. Purchasing, restoring purchases and downloading require an internet connection and the device’s Google Play account. Google handles payment; the app does not receive your card details. The publisher does not run a licence server or receive your dimensions or calculation sheets. The app contains no advertising, publisher-operated analytics or custom diagnostic SDK.

According to Google’s documentation, module delivery processes device and app-version metadata to deliver the correct module and retain it during updates. Google Play processes store and purchase data under its own terms and privacy policy. Links to Google’s privacy policy and Play Feature Delivery’s data-safety information appear below.

Copying, exporting and contacting support

Copying a sheet writes to the Android clipboard. Exporting uses the system file picker and saves text to the destination you choose. If you select an installed cloud provider, its terms and privacy policy apply to the file you save there. The app does not gain general access to your files.

If you choose to email support, the publisher receives your message and attachments to answer your enquiry. Do not attach information you do not want to share. The app does not send messages or diagnostics automatically. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry. You may request their deletion at the same email address, unless retention is legally required.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual sheets from Saved after confirmation. To remove preferences and recent inputs as well, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it. The app disables cloud backup and transfer of its data. Clearing storage or uninstalling also removes the local receipt; the purchase remains associated with Google Play and can be restored online. Exported TXT files and copies saved elsewhere remain there until you delete them.

Permissions and use

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or general storage permissions. Android grants access only to the export file you select. There is no app account to close or subscription to cancel. Internet access is used for Google Play integration. Calculations run locally, and Pro that has already been activated and downloaded does not expire for lack of an internet connection.

Questions and changes

For privacy or support enquiries, email thegastry@gmail.com. If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy and the store declarations will be updated before that version is distributed.
