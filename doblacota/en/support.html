Checking a calculation

Tell us the method, input dimensions, unit and centerline radius. In the calculator, open the formula and calculation breakdown. You may export a TXT sheet and attach it to your email if you wish; it is never sent automatically.

Units and radius

Change units in the calculator. The same centerline radius is used in the angle search and every output dimension. Older sheets are flagged for review and recalculated when opened. Select a bender or enter a measured physical radius.

Sheets and connectivity

Calculations are saved on your device first. Drive merges changes when you are online; Android may delay background syncing.

Google Drive · Pro. Backup is optional. Connecting merges this device’s saved calculations with those in the selected account.

Recovery creates a new calculation and keeps your current ones. These versions are stored on this device, including those received from Drive.

Restoring Pro

Open Settings, Doblacota Pro and Restore purchase using the Google Play account that made the purchase. If the module is missing, tap Download Pro when online. An interrupted download does not remove the purchase. Do not pay again while a payment is pending; wait for Google Play’s confirmation. Never send card numbers, passwords or a complete purchase token to support.

Results and reference tables

Incompatible inputs produce an explanation. Check units and radius. Catalogues contain reference data, not manufacturer-certified specifications. The breakdown distinguishes calculations using a physical radius from the rounded multiplier method.

Reporting a problem

Include your Android version, app version, steps and observed result. Where possible, include a screenshot and the expected result. Check attachments before sending them. The app does not collect crash reports automatically.
