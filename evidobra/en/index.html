Evidobra privacy policy

Version 1.0.0 · Revised: 6 November 2026.

Controller and contact

Juan Mauricio Piano Monzó, Spain. Privacy and support contact: gastry@gmail.com. This policy applies to the Evidobra Android app, package com.gastry.evidobra.

Data you enter

Projects, issues, assignees, contact details, written locations, photos, drawn signatures, templates, history, preferences and drafts are stored in the app’s private space on your device. Locations are entered manually, not obtained from GPS. The controller does not receive this content from the app.

Optional Google Drive connection

Evidobra works locally without a mandatory account. You can authorize your Google account to save, view, delete and restore complete backups in Evidobra’s private space within your Drive. Only the drive.appdata permission is requested; it does not allow access to your other Drive files. Google processes content and the connection under its terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Backups use the available storage in your account. Projects are not sent to the controller’s servers. The app does not include ads, analytics or advertising identifiers.

Automatic Drive backups

These are off when installed. If enabled, Android will attempt to upload a complete backup each day when a connection and sufficient battery are available. An unmetered network, usually Wi-Fi, is used by default; you can allow other connections. Android can postpone background work, and no exact time is guaranteed. The latest seven automatic backups from each installation are kept; manual backups remain until you delete them. You can view and delete these backups from Evidobra. Disconnecting Drive stops new transfers but does not delete existing backups; you can revoke permission in your Google account. Changes are not automatically synchronized between devices.

Automatic saving of changes to Drive

You can also enable optional incremental protection: records and images are saved separately in Evidobra’s private space in your Drive. When you enable it, you choose whether content deleted within the app stays in Drive or is removed from the current saved data. Previous versions are kept for recovery and continue to use storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data through Android does not request deletion from Drive. Without a network connection, available storage or authorization, pending changes remain only on the device; the app displays their status and can notify you if you allow alerts. Android may delay uploads. A complete save is reported only after verification. Restoring a version requires confirmation and replaces local data. Changes are not automatically merged between devices. This feature does not alter previous ZIP backups.

Authorization and connection data

The selected account email, backup preferences, a random identifier for this installation and the last backup status are stored locally. The identifier separates backup rotation for different devices. Google Play services manage authorization and permission renewal; Evidobra does not request your Google password or store access tokens in ZIP files. Internet permission is used for these optional Drive features.

Photos, camera and documents

The app uses Android pickers to import only the images or backups you choose. The camera opens through the system camera app. Evidobra does not request general access to your gallery, location, contacts or microphone. Original imported photo files are kept; they may contain metadata such as capture date or location. Review this metadata before sharing a ZIP backup containing originals.

Exports and backups

You can generate PDF, CSV, Excel, images and ZIP backups. Saving or sharing delivers a copy to the destination you choose through Android, such as another app, a folder or a cloud provider. That destination applies its own terms and privacy policy. ZIP backups are not encrypted; protect them and share them only with appropriate people. Android system backup is disabled for Evidobra.

Retention and deletion

Local content is kept until you delete it in the app, clear app data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Drafts and images from a new edit can be discarded in the editor. Deleting a project removes its issues and associated media. Files exported outside the app must be deleted at their destination. Deleting a project or uninstalling does not delete Drive backups; delete them separately in Evidobra or manage app data in Google Drive. Before changing devices or uninstalling, save a ZIP backup that you can restore.

Purchases and support

Evidobra is offered as a one-time purchase to download it from Google Play. The applicable price is shown in the store before payment. Google Play may verify the installation and license; these checks may periodically require an Internet connection. If you purchase through Google Play, Google handles payment and receipts under its privacy policy; Evidobra does not receive or store card numbers. If you email gastry@gmail.com, the controller receives your message, address and any files you choose to attach to handle your request. Email is provided by Gmail (Google). Do not send third-party photos or reports unless needed. Support messages are kept as long as necessary to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

Your rights

You manage local content on your device; the controller does not have a remote copy to view or delete. To request access, correction, deletion or other rights concerning data you have sent to support, email gastry@gmail.com. You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Changes

If features that transmit data are added, this policy and store declarations will be updated before those features are included. The app keeps work local and offers optional Drive backups, without synchronizing changes between devices or team members.
